Match Manager

The offline match-day app for junior football coaches and team managers, on iPhone and iPad.

New to coaching or managing a junior team? Match Manager does the juggling for you: who plays where, who sits out next, and a fair share of the game for every player. It works for any team from under-6s to under-16s, whatever format you play, and it runs on your phone or iPad, so it works at grounds with no signal.

Made for every junior team

Whether it's your first season or you've just put your hand up to manage your child's team, Match Manager works for any team up to and including under-16s. Choose 4 v 4, 7 v 7, 9 v 9 or 11 v 11, each with its own formations, and a match from 30 to 90 minutes. In Australia, pick your age group in setup and it fills in the format and match length most competitions use.

  • 4 v 4

    Under 6 and 7, no keeper
    2 × 20 minutes

  • 7 v 7

    Under 8 and 9
    2 × 20 minutes

  • 9 v 9

    Under 10 to 13
    2 × 25 or 2 × 30 minutes

  • 11 v 11

    Under 14 to 16
    2 × 35 or 2 × 40 minutes

The usual ages and match lengths in Australian junior competitions. Associations differ, so you can change any of them.

How a match runs

Here's a match in Match Manager, from setting up to full time. These are the app's own screens on iPhone, with a made-up team.

  1. Set up in minutes

    Add your players and choose your format. Or look around a sample season first, and press START to watch a match run.

  2. Plan before kick-off

    The app deals players into sub rotation groups with a mix of positions. Tap a quarter to see its line-up, and swap anyone you like.

  3. Know when to change

    When a quarter ends, an alert says who comes off and who goes on, and Make the swap does it in one tap.

  4. Subs in two taps

    Quick Rotate brings on whoever has played least, in positions they like. Check the changes, then confirm.

  5. Fair minutes for all

    At full time, see how long everyone played and where. Across the season, the app evens out the minutes.

The whole match on one screen

On iPad you see the clock, the field, the sub rotation groups and every substitution side by side, so the whole match is in view at once.

Everything you need on match day

Made for volunteer coaches and team managers who run junior matches from the sideline and want every player to get a fair share of the game.

  • Set up in a few minutes

    Choose one match or a whole season, pick your age group, add your players and check the match settings. A sample season lets you look around first.

  • Every junior team

    4 v 4, 7 v 7, 9 v 9 and 11 v 11, each with its own formations, from under-6s to under-16s, and games from 30 to 90 minutes.

  • Fair game time

    Sub rotation groups and a phase planner, with game time tracked for every player and every position.

  • Bench and substitutions

    Track every change, with undo, plus fatigue warnings and audio alerts.

  • A timer you can trust

    The match timer keeps time from the clock, so it stays right even if your screen locks.

  • The whole season

    Fixtures, goals, match and player notes, a post-match review, statistics and a formation heat map.

  • Backups that last

    A backup is saved after every match, and every later version can restore it. You see a preview before anything is replaced.

  • Help when you need it

    Built-in help and tooltips explain how things work.

Works with no signal

Match Manager doesn't need the internet. Everything runs on your phone or iPad, so it's ready at any ground, whatever the reception.

Your team stays on your phone

There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Players' names and notes stay on your device, and we never see them. Read our privacy policy.

Try three matches free

Match Manager is free to download, and your first three matches are free. After that, one purchase (A$14.99 in Australia) unlocks unlimited matches. There's no subscription, and Family Sharing is included. Your teams, past matches and backups stay available either way.
