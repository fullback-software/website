Help us help you

When you email about a problem, please tell us:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and its iOS or iPadOS version
  • the Match Manager version, which you'll find in the app
  • what you did, what happened and what you expected
  • anything else that helps, such as a screenshot.

Keep players' details private

Please don't send us players' names or notes. If a backup file would help us fix a problem, we'll ask first, use it only for that, and delete it afterwards.

Common questions

Does Match Manager need an internet connection?

No. It works fully offline, so it's ready at grounds with no signal.

Where is my team's information kept?

Only on your device, inside the app. We never receive it. Our privacy policy has the details.

How do I back up my team or move it to a new device?

Save a backup in the app. On iPhone and iPad it also saves one after every match, in the Auto backups folder, keeping the newest ten. Backups appear in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad), in the Match Manager folder. Copy the backup somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive, or send it to your new device. To restore it, open the Settings tab and tap Restore from Backup, under Backups. You'll see a preview before anything is replaced.

How much does Match Manager cost?

It's free to download, and your first three matches are free. The sample team and the 5-minute practice run never count towards them. After that, one purchase unlocks unlimited matches. There's no subscription, and nothing you've entered is ever locked.

How do I get my purchase back on a new iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Settings tab, tap Help & user guide, and tap Restore purchase at the bottom. With Family Sharing, people in your family group can unlock it too without buying it again.

Will deleting the app delete my data?

Yes. Deleting Match Manager removes its data from your device, including any backups saved in its folder. Copy the backups you want to keep somewhere else first.
