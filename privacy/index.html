Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version

  • Match Manager keeps your team's information on your device. We never receive it.
  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it works offline.
  • If you email us, we use what you send only to help you.
  • This website doesn't use cookies or analytics.

About this policy

This policy explains how Fullback Software ("we" or "us") handles information in the Match Manager app, when you contact us, and on this website. Fullback Software is an Australian business, ABN 49 373 001 951.

The Match Manager app

What you enter stays on your device

Match Manager stores the information you enter inside the app on your device. That includes your team's name and crest, players' names and availability, formations, game time, substitutions, goals, notes, fixtures and statistics. The app works without an internet connection and doesn't send this information to us or anyone else, so we can't see it.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

You don't need an account to use Match Manager. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. We don't collect any data from the app.

Photos

If you add a team crest, you choose the image and the app keeps a copy with your team. The app only receives the image you pick.

Backups and sharing

When you save a backup, and automatically after each match, the app creates a file in its own folder on your device, which you can see in the Files app. It keeps the ten newest automatic backups. The app also keeps a copy of your season in its own storage on your device, so it survives if your device runs low on space. If you share a backup or match details from the app, they go only where you choose to send them, and that app or service's own privacy terms apply.

Alerts

Any alerts Match Manager shows are scheduled on your device. We don't use a notification service.

Your device's own backups

If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, those backups can include Match Manager's data. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Players' information

Match Manager is designed for coaches, not children. A coach can enter junior players' names and notes about them. That information stays on the coach's device and we never receive it. We suggest recording only what you need, such as first names. If you're a parent with a question about what a coach has recorded, please ask the coach.

Deleting your information

You can change or remove information in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, including backups saved in its folder. Copies you've saved elsewhere, such as in iCloud Drive, stay there until you delete them.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you send. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems or improve Match Manager. We won't add you to a mailing list.

If we ask for a backup file to help fix a problem, we'll use it only for that and delete it once the problem is sorted.

Our email is provided by Google Workspace, which stores it on our behalf. We keep support emails only as long as we need them, and we'll delete yours if you ask.

App Store downloads and purchases

Apple handles downloads and any purchases under its own privacy policy, and we don't receive your payment details. To check whether you've unlocked unlimited matches, the app asks Apple on your device, and that check sends nothing to us. Apple gives us reports, such as download and sales numbers, that don't identify you. If you've agreed in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also send us crash reports and usage statistics, which don't identify you either.

This website

fullbacksoftware.com.au doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it doesn't load anything from other websites. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See About GitHub Pages and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. The app doesn't share data with anyone, because it doesn't collect any.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, which is usually just your emails to us, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email support@fullbacksoftware.com.au and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us so we can put it right.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Match Manager or this website handles information, we'll update this policy and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
