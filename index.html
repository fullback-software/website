Match Manager

The offline match-day app for junior football coaches and team managers, for any team from under-6s to under-16s, in any format from 4 v 4 to 11 v 11. Plan rotations, track everyone's game time and run the bench from the sideline, even with no signal.

Find out more

Why nine dots?

Nine dots make the F in our logo. They're all the same size because every kid gets a fair go, whether their team plays 4 v 4 or 11 v 11. The orange dot at the bottom is the fullback, the player at the back.
